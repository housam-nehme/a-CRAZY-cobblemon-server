Rules

Discord Rules

  1. Be Respectful
    • Respect everyone and do not be toxic. Any intentional harassment, racism, sexism, homophobia, or transphobia will result in a mute or ban.
    • Absolutely NO slurs or signs of hate speech, regardless of arguments or disagreements. You will be kicked or banned immediately.
  2. Swearing / Edgy humor
    • swearing is fine, everyone here is old enough to take big boy words, but know your limits, and don't be rude or disrespectful.
    • edgy humor is generally okay, but read the room, if it becomes annoying, malicious, or makes people feel uncomfortable, you will most be muted or kicked.

Minecraft Rules

Our world is full of creative and talented trainers. These are the rules for our Minecraft server in order to keep it that way.

  1. No Griefing
    • We do not allow any sort of destruction of another player's land, farm, or any type of building.
    • This includes killing other players' pets or farm animals (without permission).
    • Please be mindful of any pranks or trolls you may do to other players.
  2. No Stealing
    • Stealing from other players' chests or farms is not allowed.
    • Please ask before taking; there should be no reason to cause issues regarding items.
    • Villagers that belong to someone are THEIRS—you must get permission from them before using them.
  3. PvP Rules
    • PvP is enabled, but do not kill other players randomly.
    • Randomly killing players will result in a blacklist.
  4. Land Claim Information
    • Please respect all land claims.
    • To keep exploration fresh, keep any important bases or farms within the 8k by 8k border.
    • Check the BlueMap or ask staff which areas have been claimed.
    • Find an open area that is not circled or claimed by anyone else.
    • Please ask before building in someone else's claimed or built-in area.
  5. Always Work for What You Need
    • People will gladly help you out, but don't let it become free handouts. Earn your gear, blocks, and resources yourself.
    • Getting free stuff kills the fun, and you'll lose motivation to keep playing much faster.
    • Set a goal, work toward it, and build up from there!
