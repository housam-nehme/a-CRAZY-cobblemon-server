Rules:

You WILL see a preview of your opponent's team right before the battle and you must lock in your team before the battle.

  • Battle Format: Double Battle (Bring 6, Choose 4)
  • Turn Limit: 20 turns maximum (results in a tie if exceeded)
  • Gimmicks: No gimmicks allowed (e.g., Tera, Mega, Dynamax)
  • BST Limit: No Pokémon with a Base Stat Total strictly above 600 (BST 600 is allowed)
  • Pokémon Category Bans: No Legendaries, Mythics, or Paradox Pokémon
  • Ability Ban: Pokémon with the ability Moody are not permitted
  • Species: Players cannot have two Pokémon with the same Pokédex number on the same team
  • Items: Players cannot have two of the same held items on their Pokémon at a time
  • OHKO: A Pokémon may not have the moves Fissure, Guillotine, Horn Drill, or Sheer Cold in its moveset
  • Sleep: Only one Pokémon may be put to sleep on the opposing team at a time, if not induced by a move such as Rest
  • Endless Battle: Any moveset on any Pokémon that is capable of intentionally causing an endless battle is banned
⚠️ IMPORTANT

Once you are #lockedin with your team/moves you can NOT change it mid way to gain an advantage. There will be moderators watching you select your team so no funny business.
